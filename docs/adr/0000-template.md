# ADR-0000: Template

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-title.md) | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who
- **Plane:** which architectural plane(s) this touches
- **Reversibility:** cheap | moderate | expensive — and what it would cost to undo

## Context

What forces are at play. What constraint made this a decision rather than a default.
State the problem, not the solution.

## Decision

What we are doing. Specific: name versions, algorithms, licence identifiers.

## Alternatives considered

For each serious alternative: what it is, and the concrete reason it was rejected.
"We liked the other one better" is not a reason. At least two alternatives.

## Consequences

### Positive

### Negative / accepted costs

### Residual risk

What could still go wrong, and what signal would tell us this decision was wrong.

## Verification status

Which claims in this ADR were counter-verified against primary sources, and which were
not. Cite sources with URLs. Mark unverified assumptions explicitly — an ADR that hides
its uncertainty is worse than one that admits it.

## Revisit when

The concrete trigger that should make us reopen this decision.
